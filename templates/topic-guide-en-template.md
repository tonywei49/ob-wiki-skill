# Topics Guide

> Topic pages are created from confirmed research directions, not guessed during initialization.

## How to Use

1. Write the real questions in `研究方向.md`.
2. After sources accumulate, ask the AI to organize a topic around one research direction.
3. Use topic pages to collect evidence, conclusions, disagreements, and open questions across sources.

## Do Not Create Topics When

- Only one source has been read.
- A keyword merely appears in a source.
- The user has not confirmed the direction is worth tracking.
